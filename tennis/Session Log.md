## How to Log a Session

After each session, tell Claude:
1. **Date**
2. **Session type** — Drill / Match / Lesson / Casual hit
3. **Duration** (minutes)
4. **Avg HR** (from Oura)
5. **Max HR** (from Oura)
6. **Calories** (from Oura)
7. **Steps** during session (from Oura, optional)
8. **Opponent + score** (if match)
9. **What you worked on** (serve, footwork, etc.) or what the focus was
10. **RPE** (1-10)
11. **Notes** — how it felt, weather, anything notable, any pain/discomfort
